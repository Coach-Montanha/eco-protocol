# Higgsfield Effects 2.0 — AI Video Skill

Use this skill when building or prompting generative AI video workflows, cinematic camera controls, and VFX.

1. **Cinema Studio Controls**:
   - Aspect Ratios: `21:9` (Cinematic Scope), `16:9` (Landscape), `9:16` (Vertical Stories/Reels).
   - Camera Paths: Pan, Tilt, Orbit, Push-In, Crane Shot, Whip Zoom.
2. **VFX & Soul 2.0 Suite**:

   - Style Transfer & Inpainting: Canvas object removal and upscaling (4K/8K).
3. **Integration**: Compatible with Gemini Omni Flash (`gemini-omni-1.1-flash`) and video generation SDKs.
